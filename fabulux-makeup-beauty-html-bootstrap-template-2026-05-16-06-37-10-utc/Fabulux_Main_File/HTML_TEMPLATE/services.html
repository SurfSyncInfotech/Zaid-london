Treatment guide

Before and after your treatment

General advice — your therapist will tailor it to you.

Lashes & Brows

Before

Tell us about allergies; arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes.

Afterwards

Avoid rubbing the eyes and follow your therapist's advice on keeping lashes and brows clean.

Manicure & Pedicure

Before

Bring inspiration photos for nail design; mention any nail conditions.

Afterwards

Moisturise hands and cuticles and avoid harsh chemicals to keep gel finishes lasting.

Hair

Before

Bring reference photos and tell your stylist about past colour or treatments.

Afterwards

Use products suited to your colour or treatment, as advised by your stylist.

Facial & Massage

Before

Share skin concerns and any medical conditions before you begin.

Afterwards

Drink water and follow your therapist's skincare advice to keep skin glowing.

Waxing

Before

Let us know about skin sensitivities; allow hair to grow to a suitable length.

Afterwards

Keep skin clean and avoid heat and friction straight afterwards.

Before you book

Treatment questions answered

How long does a treatment take?
Timings depend on the treatment and your chosen look. Our reception team will confirm the time needed when you book.
Can I combine treatments in one visit?
Yes — many guests pair hair with nails, or a facial with a massage. Tell us what you would like when you book so we can plan your time.
How do I prepare for a colour or highlights appointment?
Arrive with clean, product-free hair if possible and bring photos of looks you like. Your stylist will talk through the options before starting.
Are lash and brow treatments suitable for sensitive skin?
Please tell us about any allergies or sensitivities when you book. Your therapist will advise you before treatment.
What aftercare will I be given?
Your therapist will give you simple aftercare advice for your treatment so you can enjoy the results for longer.
Do you offer treatments for men and children?
Yes — men's classic and deluxe manicures, children's nail design, and children's wash, cut and blow-dry.
How do I find out prices?
Prices vary by treatment. Call +44(0)74 1555 5531 or use the enquiry form and we will advise.
