Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?
We are open every day of the week, Monday to Sunday, from 10am to 10pm.
Where are you located?
We are at 47 Beauchamp Place, London SW3 1NX.
How do I book an appointment?
Call us on +44 (0)7415555531, email Reception@ziadlondon.uk, or send a request through the booking form and our reception team will confirm your appointment.
Which treatments do you offer?
Lashes & brows, manicure & pedicure, hair, facials & massage and waxing — see the full list on our services page.
Do you offer a VIP room and hijab services?
Yes. We have a VIP room and hijab services available. Please contact reception to arrange them for your visit.
Do you treat men and children?
Yes — we offer classic and deluxe manicures for men, plus children's nail design and children's wash, cut and blow-dry.
How much do treatments cost?
Prices vary by treatment. Please call +44 (0)7415555531 or send us an enquiry and we will be happy to advise.
