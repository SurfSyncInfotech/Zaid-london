Before You Enquire

Help us help you

The more we know, the faster our reception team can arrange your appointment. It helps to have these to hand:

  • Your name and a contact number
  • The treatment(s) you are interested in
  • Your preferred day and time
  • Any allergies or sensitivities
  • Photos of the look you would like (by email)

Questions about getting in touch

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?
Our reception team replies as soon as they can during opening hours. For anything urgent, please call us.
Can I change or cancel my appointment?
Please call +44(0)74 1555 5531 or email Reception@ziadlondon.uk as early as possible and we will help.
Do I need to book in advance?
We recommend booking so we can reserve your preferred time. Call reception to ask about availability.
How do I get to the salon?
We are at 47 Beauchamp Place, London SW3 1NX. Use the map above for directions.
Can I send photos of the look I want?
Yes — email them to Reception@ziadlondon.uk and mention them in your enquiry.
Who do I contact about group or special-occasion bookings?
Please call +44(0)74 1555 5531 with your date and requirements and we will help plan your visit.
