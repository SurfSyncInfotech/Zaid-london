Privacy Policy

How Ziad London handles the information you share with us.

Ziad London Hair & Beauty Salon, 47 Beauchamp Place, London SW3 1NX, respects your privacy. This page explains how we use the information you give us through this website.

Information we collect

If you use our booking or enquiry form, you provide your name, telephone number, preferred treatment, preferred date and any message you choose to write. When you submit the form, your details are sent by email to our reception team at Reception@ziadlondon.uk through an email-forwarding service (FormSubmit). If that fails, the form opens your own email application addressed to us instead.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your appointment.
  • To contact you about your booking.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it where necessary to provide our services or where required by law.

Third-party services

This website loads maps and photographs from third-party providers (such as Google Maps, Unsplash and FormSubmit) and links to our social media profiles. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting Reception@ziadlondon.uk or calling +44 (0)7415555531.
