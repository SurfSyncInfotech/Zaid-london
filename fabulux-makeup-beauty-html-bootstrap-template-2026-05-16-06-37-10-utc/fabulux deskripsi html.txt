Fabulux is a modern and elegant HTML5 Bootstrap template designed for Makeup Artists, Beauty Salons, Spas, and other beauty-related businesses. This creative template is perfect for showcasing your beauty services, makeup portfolios, beauty blogs, skincare products, cosmetic stores, and more.Whether you're running a beauty salon, freelance makeup business, or beauty academy, Fabulux provides a clean, stylish layout with a professional design and smooth user experience, helping you present your services and products in the most appealing way possible.

<ul>
<li> Powered by Bootstrap
