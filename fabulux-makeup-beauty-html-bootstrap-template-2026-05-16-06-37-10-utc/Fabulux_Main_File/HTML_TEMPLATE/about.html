About us

Questions about Ziad London

What makes Ziad London different?
We combine hair, nails, lashes, skin, massage and waxing under one roof, delivered by a team known for expertise and attention to detail — with a private VIP room and hijab services for complete comfort.
Do you only offer treatments for women?
No. We offer classic and deluxe manicures for men and children's treatments too. Please ask reception if you would like something that is not listed.
Can I arrange the VIP room or hijab services?
Yes — both are available. Please call reception or send an enquiry so we can arrange them for your visit.
Which products do you use?
We use premium, safe products to give the best results for your skin and hair. If you have allergies or sensitivities, please tell your therapist before your treatment.
Can I follow your work online?
Of course — find us on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
I am visiting London — can I still book?
Absolutely. We are at 47 Beauchamp Place, SW3 1NX, and open every day until 10pm. Call +44(0)74 1555 5531 to arrange a time.
